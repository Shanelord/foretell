Data notes / items to confirm

Government investment starts at $125,000,000,000 and counts up at $1,000/sec. Private investment starts at $600,000,000,000 and counts up at $5,000/sec. Refreshing resets animated baselines.

The 2041 citizen funding milestone (’41) marks the 15-year horizon pending confirmation. ’29, ’33, ’36, and ’41 denote 2029–2041; figures are counts in millions of people.

Readiness scores are computed on a 0–100 scale (LOW < 40, MOD 40–69, HIGH 70+) from model telemetry and interactive scenario sliders inside each drawer.

Human / Machine GDP Split Equality tracks the dynamic 50.0% H / 50.0% M crossover calculated from device date to October 1, 2038, shifting with readiness levers. World totals add an estimated 10% for the rest of the world, and state figures use placeholder weights.

Initiatives

// Core transition initiatives, research tracks & roadmap programs

Human / Machine GDP Split Equality

What happens when machines produce as much as people do? We track the shift from human-labor GDP toward Machine Domestic Product (GMDP), counting down to the 50/50 parity benchmark, and show who's ready when it arrives. Explore the clock→.

Economic & Offset Readiness

Evaluating citizen offset capacity, unfunded gap dynamics, and public funding coverage across transition milestones.

Labor, Education & Grid Readiness

Monitoring job displacement velocity, workforce & skill alignment, layoff cushions, and data center grid/power capacity.
